{{ val(business.legal_name, 'registered business name') }} ("NTS") runs this site. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, mobile number, an optional email address, and your password, which we store only as a one-way hash.
  • Orders: delivery addresses, the items you bought, and your payment status. Your phone number and saved addresses are encrypted in our database.
  • On your device: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, and your cart, which is saved in your browser.

We do not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank login details. Razorpay handles all of these.

Why we use it

We use your data to verify your account, process and deliver your orders, handle cancellations, returns and refunds, and reply when you contact us. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay: processes your payments and refunds.
  • Shiprocket and its courier partners: receive your name, address and phone number so they can deliver your order and collect returns.
  • Twilio: sends the one-time passwords (OTPs) to your phone.
  • Our hosting and database providers, who store the data on our behalf.
  • Government authorities, but only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. We keep order and invoice records for the period tax law requires.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in Account. To get a copy of your data, have it erased, or withdraw your consent, write to {{ val(business.email, 'support email') }}.
